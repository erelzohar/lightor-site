that prefer to read Arabic: Israeli towns, prices in shekels. Written in
Arabic for Arabic searches (فصحى مبسطة, plural address), never translated
from the Hebrew posts. Plan: `Projects/lightor-blog-languages-plan.md`,
section 4 (approved by Erel on 2026-11-24).
