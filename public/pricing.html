60 days of Lightor+ free

Every new account gets Lightor+ for its first 60 days after signup, free and with no credit card. You can subscribe at any time, including during those 60 days - the first charge is made only when they end. Subscribing after the free period is charged right away. If you don't subscribe, the account moves to the free plan when the 60 days end, and your site stays online.

  • The subscription renews automatically every month or every year. Cancel any time from the dashboard (Account Settings → Subscription) or from Paddle's customer portal; your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and nothing more is charged.
